# Contributing to Lu

Thanks for helping. Lu is licensed under the AGPL-3.0, and DialectForge LLC also offers it under a commercial license. To keep both possible, **every contributor has to agree to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md)** before a pull request can be merged. You keep your copyright. The CLA hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, and it says so.

## How to agree

Either put this line in your pull request description:

> I have read the Lu CLA in CLA.md and I agree to it for this and all my future contributions to Lu.

or add yourself to [`CLA-SIGNATURES.md`](CLA-SIGNATURES.md) in your first pull request.

## Guidelines

- Keep pull requests focused, and say what changed and why.
- Run `cargo test` and keep it green. Add tests for fixes.
- Never commit secrets, keys, brain files or personal data.
- Be honest in docs and PR descriptions: say what was tested and what wasn't.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. See `AGENTS.md`. Questions: info@dialectforge.com
